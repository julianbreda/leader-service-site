The Maintenance Program

One membership. Your whole property, maintained.

You’re either on the Leader Service maintenance program or you’re not — no tiers to compare, no packages to decode. We walk your property, scope the exact systems that need care, and set one straightforward price based on what you actually have.

Like your health, the systems that keep a property running last longer with regular care than with emergency repairs. Every visit is documented, so you always have a clear record of what’s been done — and what’s coming up.

FAQ

Maintenance program questions

How does the maintenance program work?

A property is either on the program or not; there are no tiers. We walk the property, inventory every mechanical system, and price the program on the systems you choose to cover, each serviced at its own correct interval.

What does the maintenance program include?

Priority dispatch, a 10% repair discount on any repair (including systems not on the program), an annual digital service report, online access to your service history, no call-out or truck charges on program work, and transfer to the new owner if you sell.

How long is the maintenance agreement?

Residential agreements are 12 months and renew automatically, with 30 days' notice to cancel. Commercial agreements are 12 or 36 months with 60 days' notice.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Every agreement can be billed annually or monthly, at the same price either way.

Do you charge a service call or truck fee?

Not on maintenance program work. For one-off service calls, we explain any diagnostic or trip charge before booking.
