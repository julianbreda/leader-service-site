A maintenance schedule for an Ontario home's mechanical systems

From the technicians at Leader Service.

The short answer

Ontario homes are easiest to maintain in two windows: fall for heating (furnace, boiler, radiant, snowmelt, humidifier, exterior shut-offs) and spring for cooling and water (air conditioning, sump pump, irrigation, drains). Tankless water heaters, softeners and filters run on their own annual or semi-annual schedule. Each system should be serviced at its own interval, not on a blanket schedule.

Fall: September to November

  • Furnace: annual inspection and cleaning
  • Boiler and radiant floors: annual service, pressure and controls
  • Snowmelt: glycol test and controls, before the first snowfall
  • Humidifier: pad or filter change
  • Exterior hose bibs and irrigation: shut off and drain before the first freeze
  • Gas fireplace: cleaning and safety check

Spring: April to June

  • Air conditioner or heat pump: coil cleaning and refrigerant check
  • Sump pump: test before and after the thaw, check the battery backup
  • Backwater valve: inspection and cleaning
  • Irrigation: start-up and backflow device check
  • Main drain: camera inspection if you have mature trees or recurring slow drains

Year-round intervals

  • Furnace filter: every one to three months
  • Tankless water heater: descale annually in hard water
  • Tank water heater: flush and anode check annually
  • Water softener and filters: per manufacturer, often every 6 to 12 months
  • HRV/ERV: filter and core cleaning, two to four times a year

Let someone else keep the calendar

On the Leader Service maintenance program, every system in the house is recorded with its own interval and batched into the fall and spring windows, so each one is serviced when it should be and documented every time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What home maintenance should be done before winter in Ontario?

Service the furnace or boiler, commission radiant and snowmelt systems, change the humidifier pad, and shut off and drain exterior hose bibs and irrigation.

How often should a furnace filter be changed?

Every one to three months, depending on filter type, pets and how often the system runs.
