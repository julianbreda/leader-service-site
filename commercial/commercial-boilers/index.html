Commercial boilers & hydronics

Even heat on every floor, documented every visit.

The short answer

Commercial boilers should have at least an annual service before the heating season, including combustion analysis, safety and operating control tests, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and checks of pumps, expansion tanks and water treatment. Larger plants usually need additional seasonal visits. Leader Service maintains boiler plants for commercial and multi-residential buildings across the GTA.

Boiler plant maintenance

Combustion testing and burner adjustment, low-water cut-off and limit control testing, relief valves, heat exchanger inspection, pumps, expansion tanks, glycol and water treatment. Results are recorded for each boiler.

Distribution and balancing

Hot-and-cold complaints usually trace back to air, pumps or balancing. We work through the distribution system zone by zone so every floor gets consistent heat without wasted energy.

Emergency response

A down boiler in January is an emergency for tenants. We respond 24/7, with priority dispatch for maintenance program clients.

Where we provide commercial boilers

From our base in Concord we serve Vaughan, Woodbridge, Maple, Kleinburg, King City, Richmond Hill, Toronto and the surrounding GTA.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How often should a commercial boiler be serviced?

At least annually before the heating season, with additional visits for larger plants or critical buildings.

Do you maintain multi-residential boiler plants?

Yes, including condominiums and rental buildings.

Can boiler service be combined with backflow and HVAC?

Yes. One maintenance agreement can cover every mechanical system in the building, scheduled around each asset's own frequency.
