Homes across Vaughan

Vaughan's housing ranges from 1970s and 1980s Woodbridge homes to the 1990s and 2000s subdivisions of Maple and Vellore and new custom builds. Furnaces and air conditioners in many of those subdivisions are reaching the end of their life, while custom homes increasingly run boilers, radiant floors, snowmelt and tankless hot water that need specialist service.

Backflow compliance in Vaughan

The City of Vaughan's Backflow Prevention By-law requires most industrial, commercial, institutional and multi-residential properties to have a backflow preventer tested annually by a qualified tester. We track each device's due date and handle the reporting.

Hard water

Like the rest of York Region, Vaughan's water is hard. Scale shortens the life of tankless heaters and boilers, which is why we recommend testing and softening in most homes.
