Water filtration & softening

Better water for the house, and everything it runs through.

The short answer

Water in York Region and much of the GTA is hard, which leaves scale in tankless heaters, boilers, fixtures and appliances. A water softener removes the hardness minerals, a whole-home filter improves taste and removes sediment and chlorine, and a reverse osmosis system provides drinking water. Homes on private wells may also need UV treatment.

Test first

We start with a water test for hardness, iron and other markers, so the system is matched to your water rather than sold from a catalogue. Homes on private wells, common in King Township and on rural lots, need a different setup than homes on municipal water.

Systems we install and service

Water softeners, whole-home carbon filtration, sediment filters, reverse osmosis drinking-water systems and UV disinfection for well water. Each is sized for your flow rate and household.

Filter service on a schedule

Filters and media only work while they are changed on time. On the maintenance program, filter changes and water testing are scheduled automatically and recorded in your service history.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Vaughan water hard?

Yes. Municipal water across York Region is generally considered hard, which is why scale builds in kettles, fixtures and tankless water heaters.

Do I need a softener if I have a tankless water heater?

It is strongly recommended. Hardness scale is the most common cause of reduced output and early failure in tankless units.

What does a homeowner on a well need?

A water test first. Many wells need sediment filtration and a softener, and UV disinfection is a common safeguard against bacteria.
