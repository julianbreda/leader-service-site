Backflow testing requirements in Vaughan

From the technicians at Leader Service.

The short answer

Under the City of Vaughan's Backflow Prevention By-law, most industrial, commercial, institutional and multi-residential properties must have a backflow prevention device on their water service and have it tested by a qualified tester at least once a year. Test results are submitted to the City. Single-family homes are generally exempt, except for devices such as lawn irrigation backflow preventers.

What a backflow preventer does

A backflow preventer stops water from inside a building flowing backwards into the public water supply when pressure drops, for example during a water main break or firefighting. Without it, chemicals, boiler water or process water could contaminate drinking water.

Who needs one

In Vaughan, the requirement generally applies to industrial, commercial, institutional and multi-residential properties. The required type of device depends on the hazard level of the property. Confirm your property's specific requirement on the City of Vaughan's Backflow Prevention Program pages.

Annual testing

Devices must be tested at least annually and after installation, repair or relocation, by a tester the City recognizes. Each device has its own anniversary date, which is why a building with several devices can have tests falling due at different times of year.

Staying compliant without chasing dates

Leader Service records every device you own and its anniversary, schedules the test before it is due, rebuilds or replaces devices that fail, and keeps the reports on file. Backflow can be a stand-alone scope or part of one maintenance agreement covering the building's mechanical systems.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much notice does the City give before a test is due?

Do not rely on a notice. Track each device's anniversary date yourself or have your service provider track it.

Does a residential irrigation system need a backflow preventer?

Yes. Lawn irrigation systems connected to the municipal supply require a backflow preventer in Vaughan.

What if my device fails its test?

It must be repaired or replaced and retested, and the passing result submitted.
