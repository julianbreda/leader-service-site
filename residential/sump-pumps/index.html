Sump pumps & backwater valves

The two devices that keep a basement dry.

The short answer

A sump pump and a backwater valve protect a basement in two different ways. The sump pump removes groundwater from around the foundation; the backwater valve stops sewage from flowing back into the house when city sewers surcharge. Both should be tested at least once a year, and a battery backup keeps the sump running during a power outage.

Sump pump service

We test the float and switch, run the pump through a full cycle, inspect the check valve and discharge line, and confirm the pit is clean. Pumps that are undersized, noisy or near the end of their life are replaced, and we add battery backup or water-powered backup pumps and high-water alarms.

Backwater valves

A normally-open backwater valve on the main sewer line closes when sewage tries to flow back into the home. We install, inspect and clean backwater valves so the flap seals when it is needed.

City subsidies

Several GTA municipalities offer subsidies for flood protection devices. In Toronto, the Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy Program covers part of the cost of backwater valves, sump pumps, battery backups and a home plumbing assessment. We provide the itemized invoice and documentation the application needs.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How often should a sump pump be tested?

At least once a year, and before the spring thaw. Pour water into the pit until the float lifts and confirm the pump starts and empties the pit.

Do I need a backwater valve?

If your home is connected to a municipal sewer and has a basement with plumbing fixtures or a floor drain, a backwater valve is the main protection against sewer backup. Older homes in Toronto and parts of the GTA are most at risk.

Does Toronto subsidize backwater valves?

Yes. Toronto's Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy Program covers part of the invoiced cost of eligible devices including backwater valves, sump pumps and battery backups. Check the City of Toronto's program page for current amounts and eligibility.
