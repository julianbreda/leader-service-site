Ontario heat pump and home energy rebates

From the technicians at Leader Service.

The short answer

In 2026, Ontario homeowners can get rebates through the Home Renovation Savings Program: up to $7,500 for a cold-climate air-source heat pump, up to $12,000 for a ground-source heat pump, $500 for a heat pump water heater and $100 for a smart thermostat. Income-qualified homes switching from oil can receive up to $25,000 through the Oil to Heat Pump Affordability program. Amounts and rules change, so confirm on the program site before you buy.

Home Renovation Savings Program

Run by Save on Energy (IESO) with Enbridge Gas, it pays rebates on qualifying upgrades for homes heated by electricity, natural gas, oil, propane or wood. Single upgrades such as a heat pump need no home energy assessment; bundles of two or more upgrades start with an assessment, which is itself rebated.

  • Cold-climate air-source heat pump: up to $7,500, based on size; the unit must be on Natural Resources Canada's qualified list
  • Ground-source heat pump: up to $12,000, based on size
  • Heat pump water heater: $500, ENERGY STAR certified
  • Smart thermostat: $100
  • Home energy assessment: $600 back when you complete a bundle
  • Insulation: up to $7,700 in a bundle, or $1,250 for attic insulation alone

Oil to Heat Pump Affordability (OHPA)

For income-qualified households heating with oil, this joint federal and Ontario program pays up to $25,000 toward switching to an electric heat pump ($15,000 federal and $10,000 provincial), plus a $250 upfront payment. It is delivered in Ontario through Save on Energy's Energy Affordability Program.

Before you buy

  • Check the current amounts and terms on the program site. Rebates change and funding can close.
  • Confirm the exact model qualifies before installation, not after.
  • Keep the invoice, model and serial numbers, and photos of the installed equipment. Applications ask for them.
  • For a heat pump, a proper heat-loss calculation matters more than the rebate: an undersized cold-climate unit will lean on backup heat all winter.

Where Leader Service fits

We size and install heat pumps, heat pump water heaters and smart thermostats, and we provide the itemized invoice and equipment details the rebate application needs. If your home has a boiler or radiant heat, we will tell you honestly whether a heat pump suits it.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Ontario heat pump rebate in 2026?

Through the Home Renovation Savings Program, up to $7,500 for a cold-climate air-source heat pump and up to $12,000 for a ground-source heat pump, depending on size. Confirm current amounts on saveonenergy.ca.

Do I need an energy audit to get a heat pump rebate in Ontario?

Not for a single upgrade. A home energy assessment is needed only when you bundle two or more upgrades, and it is rebated.

Is there a rebate for switching from oil to a heat pump?

Yes. Income-qualified households can receive up to $25,000 through the Oil to Heat Pump Affordability program.
