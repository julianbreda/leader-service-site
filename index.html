FAQ

Questions homeowners and property managers ask

What areas does Leader Service serve?

Vaughan (including Woodbridge, Maple, Kleinburg, Concord and Thornhill), King City and King Township, Richmond Hill, Toronto and the surrounding Greater Toronto Area.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. No-heat, burst pipe, drain backup and flood emergencies are answered 24/7 at 647.848.7526.

Do you serve both homes and commercial buildings?

Yes. Leader Home Services covers residential properties and Leader Mechanical Services covers commercial, industrial, institutional and multi-residential buildings.

How is Leader Service related to Leader Mechanical?

Leader Service is the service and maintenance company of the Leader family of businesses. Leader Mechanical has installed mechanical systems in GTA homes and buildings for three generations; Leader Service maintains and repairs them.

How does the maintenance program work?

A property is either on the program or not; there are no tiers. We walk the property, inventory every mechanical system, and price the program on the systems you choose to cover, each serviced at its own correct interval.

What does the maintenance program include?

Priority dispatch, a 10% repair discount on any repair (including systems not on the program), an annual digital service report, online access to your service history, no call-out or truck charges on program work, and transfer to the new owner if you sell.
